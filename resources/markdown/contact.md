# Contact Us

We're a small team, so email is the best way to reach us.

**General questions, feedback, or account help**
[jupyterdocs@gmail.com](mailto:jupyterdocs@gmail.com)

**Copyright or content-ownership concerns**
[jupyterdocs@gmail.com](mailto:jupyterdocs@gmail.com) — see our [Copyright Policy](/copyright) for what to include in your report.

**Something wrong with a specific document?**
Use the "Report" button on that document's page. It goes straight to our moderation queue and is usually the fastest way to get an issue resolved.

We try to respond to emails within a few business days. We don't have social media accounts yet — check back here for updates.
